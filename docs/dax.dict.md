# DAX Dictionary

This document lists the key DAX measures used in the VoltNest Power BI dashboard.

| Measure                         | Purpose                                                                            |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue                   | Calculates net revenue after discounts and refunds.                                |
| Total Orders                    | Counts distinct orders.                                                            |
| Total Customers                 | Counts distinct customers.                                                         |
| AOV                             | Calculates average revenue per order.                                              |
| Session-to-Purchase Rate        | Measures the percentage of sessions that resulted in purchases.                    |
| CTR                             | Measures the percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks.                    |
| ROAS                            | Measures revenue generated per unit of marketing spend for cost-tracked campaigns. |
| Repeat Customer Rate            | Measures the percentage of customers who made repeat purchases.                    |
| Add-to-Cart Rate                | Measures the percentage of product-view sessions that added an item to the cart.   |
| Checkout Completion Rate        | Measures the percentage of checkout sessions that resulted in purchases.           |
| Discount Rate                   | Measures discounts as a percentage of gross revenue.                               |
| Refund Rate                     | Measures refunds as a percentage of gross revenue.                                 |
| Average Days to Second Purchase | Calculates the average time between a customer's first and second purchases.       |

These measures support the Executive Summary, Acquisition & Marketing Performance, and Conversion & Customer Retention dashboard pages.
